About New Beginning Hospital

A Sanctuary of Healing, New Life, and Compassionate Medicine

At New Beginning Hospital, we believe every medical journey—especially the arrival of a precious newborn—deserves the utmost dignity, clinical brilliance, and heartfelt empathy. Founded with the mission to elevate maternal, neonatal, and family healthcare, our hospital is purposefully engineered with world-class facilities.

From our cutting-edge Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) equipped with modern incubator suites to our sterile, laminar flow surgical theatres, our multidisciplinary team of obstetricians, pediatricians, surgeons, and nurses work round the clock to ensure you and your loved ones receive unparalleled medical attention.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions about our hospital timings, delivery packages, NICU admissions, and appointments.

New Beginning Hospital is open 24 hours a day from Monday through Saturday. Our emergency room, labor and delivery rooms, and NICU operate continuously day and night. We are closed on Sundays for routine OPD consultations, but our emergency trauma and on-call maternity teams remain on active standby.

You can reach us directly at our 24/7 helpline +92 301 0840101 (via direct phone call or WhatsApp). You can also email us at newbeginninghospital@gmail.com or click the "Book Appointment" button on this website to reserve your slot online.

Our Level-III NICU is fully equipped with temperature-regulated microprocessor incubators, radiant warmers, continuous multi-parameter cardiac monitors, double-surface LED phototherapy for jaundice, and neonatal resuscitation units. Qualified neonatologists and pediatric nurses monitor infants 24 hours a day.

Our normal delivery and C-Section packages include the delivery room/operating theater charges, gynecologist and pediatrician fee, initial newborn examination, standard medicines, routine lab tests, and hospital stay in either general wards or private VIP suites depending on your chosen package. Contact +92 301 0840101 for detailed pricing.
